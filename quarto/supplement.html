Notes on the supplement

These are kept above as printed and listed so this analysis’s code lists can be compared with them. How each was handled is on the Code lists page and in the linked issues.

  • Cryptococcosis lists the ICD-10-CM category B45 itself as well as its subcodes. ICD-10-CM has no codes B45.4 to B45.6, so the subcodes listed are all of them.
  • Diabetes marks 250, E08, E09, E10, E11 and E13 with an asterisk, meaning every code beginning with them; the other codes are matched exactly (#8).
  • Procedures are ICD procedure codes only. The paper’s Methods mention CPT codes, which the supplement does not list (#5). There is no separate row for the ventriculoperitoneal shunts the paper reports; the shunt codes are among the neurosurgical procedures (#8).
  • Medications are listed as HCPCS billing codes for cyclosporine and tacrolimus. The paper describes its drug data both ways: its Methods say drug information at UNOS registration and the first post-transplant visit “was obtained using relevant billing codes”, while the title of its Table 2 names the UNOS registration and follow-up appointment. This analysis uses the UNOS records (#2).
  • Some ICD-9 procedure codes have only three digits (031, 034, 321, 326 and 329). These are complete codes with no subcodes, and are matched exactly. 022 was also a complete code until October 2012, when it was split into 02.21 and 02.22, both of which are listed too.

-   **Diabetes** marks 250, E08, E09, E10, E11 and E13 with an asterisk, meaning every code beginning with them; the other codes are matched exactly ([#8](https://github.com/VagishHemmige/Fiorica-cryptoccoccus-analysis/issues/8)).
-   **Procedures** are ICD procedure codes only. The paper's Methods mention CPT codes, which the supplement does not list ([#5](https://github.com/VagishHemmige/Fiorica-cryptoccoccus-analysis/issues/5)). There is no separate row for the ventriculoperitoneal shunts the paper reports; the shunt codes are among the neurosurgical procedures ([#8](https://github.com/VagishHemmige/Fiorica-cryptoccoccus-analysis/issues/8)).
-   **Medications** are listed as HCPCS billing codes for cyclosporine and tacrolimus. The paper describes its drug data both ways: its Methods say drug information at UNOS registration and the first post-transplant visit "was obtained using relevant billing codes", while the title of its Table 2 names the UNOS registration and follow-up appointment. This analysis uses the UNOS records ([#2](https://github.com/VagishHemmige/Fiorica-cryptoccoccus-analysis/issues/2)).
-   Some ICD-9 procedure codes have only three digits (031, 034, 321, 326 and 329). These are complete codes with no subcodes, and are matched exactly. 022 was also a complete code until October 2012, when it was split into 02.21 and 02.22, both of which are listed too.
