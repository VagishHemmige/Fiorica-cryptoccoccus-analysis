Notes on the published results

These are kept above as printed. Each is listed so that this analysis’s outputs can be compared with what was intended. Every percentage was recomputed from its count and column size.

How percentages are printed

  • Most percentages are cut to one decimal place rather than rounded: for example, 204 of 280 (72.86%) is printed 72.8, and 449 of 985 (45.58%) is printed 45.5. This analysis’s tables round, so many of its percentages will differ from the published ones by 0.1 even when the counts agree.
  • Where a variable has missing values, percentages use the people with known values, although the table gives a denominator only for CMV serostatus and pretransplant dialysis. For example, the race rows sum to 1,263, not 1,265, and 655 of 983 (66.6%) matches the printed White survivor percentage where 655 of 985 (66.5%) would not; Hispanic ethnicity implies about 1,243 people with known ethnicity. In Table 2, the percentages imply about 1,234 people with drug records at transplant and about 1,068 at follow-up.

Table 1

  • Lung procedures: the survivor and death cells appear swapped. 84 (30) fits the 280 deaths (84/280 is 30.0%), and 270 (27.4) fits the 985 survivors (270/985 is 27.4%); read as printed (84/985 and 270/280), they would be 8.5% and 96.4%. The total, 354, is their sum.
  • Rejection episodes, survivors: 144 is printed as 14.2%; 144/985 is 14.6%. A denominator that gives 14.2% would be over 1,000, more than the 985 survivors. The total (178, 14%) and the deaths (34, 12.1%) match their counts.
  • US region: the four regions sum to 1,257 of 1,265; the table has no row for the other 8. This analysis shows Outside and Unknown as their own rows (plan §5, #12).
  • Continuous variables: the Methods describe continuous variables as mean ± SD; time since the last transplant is given as median (IQR), and the number of transplants as median (min–max), without a comparison by group.

Table 2

  • MMF at transplant, total: 1,121 is printed as 90.2%. The row’s own survivor and death percentages imply about 958 and 275 people with records, so 1,121 of about 1,233 (90.9%). The total is an average of the two groups weighted by their size, so with about 3.5 survivors for each death it should lie near the survivors’ 91.1%, not near the deaths’ 90.1%.

Text and tables

  • The Results give male sex in survivors as 66.3% and CMV seropositivity as 70.7%; Table 1 gives 66.2% and 70.6%, the same counts cut rather than rounded.
  • The Methods take immunosuppression from “the first post-transplant visit”, obtained “using relevant billing codes”; the Results and Discussion refer to a 1-year follow-up. This analysis uses the UNOS records and the earliest follow-up report (plan §5, #2).
  • The Methods name CPT codes for procedures; Supplementary Table 1 lists ICD procedure codes (plan §5, #5).
  • The Limitations say cases were identified by 2 ICD-9 codes; the case definition in Supplementary Table 1 also lists ICD-10-CM codes (plan §5).
